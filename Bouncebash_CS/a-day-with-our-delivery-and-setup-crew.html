Behind the scenes

A day with our delivery and setup crew

Follow a typical event day, from loading the truck to the last pick-up.

Aug 2026 | 3 min read | By the Bounce Bash PH team

Parties look effortless because a lot happens before guests arrive. Here is a typical day.

Morning: load and inspect

The crew loads the inflatable, ball pit, balls and balloon décor, and checks everything against your booking.

Arrival: setup

We arrive ahead of your start time to set up, style the balloons and check the play area.

Party time: supervision

Our team stays on site to supervise the play area while you enjoy your guests.

After: pick-up

Once the party wraps up, we deflate, pack and pick everything up, so you do not have to clean up the bounce house.

Delivery, setup and pick-up are part of every rental.
