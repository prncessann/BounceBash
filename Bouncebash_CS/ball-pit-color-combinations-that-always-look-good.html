Ball pit color combinations that always look good

Mix up to three colors from our palette. Here are combinations that work every time.

Jul 2026 | 3 min read | By the Bounce Bash PH team

You can mix up to three ball colors per ball pit. Our palette: White, Cream, Champagne, Blush, Sage, Mocha, Sky and Lavender.

Soft & Dreamy

Blush, Cream and White. A gentle choice for a girls' birthday or a christening.

Garden Party

Sage, Cream and Champagne. Looks lovely outdoors.

Boho Earth

Mocha, Cream and Blush. Warm and photo-ready.

Cool Skies

Sky, White and Lavender. Fresh and calm, great for boys' parties.

Tip

Pick one main color and use the others as accents. Match the balls to your balloon garland and cake table, and the whole setup feels planned.
