The complete guide to planning a stress-free kids' party

From choosing a package to picking ball colors, here is everything to sort out before your event date.

Oct 2026 | 6 min read | By the Bounce Bash PH team

A good kids' party is mostly decided weeks before the day itself. Work through these steps in order and the day will feel calm, even with a full guest list.

1. Start with the date

Open our availability calendar first. Green dates are open, pink are pending and cream are booked. Weekends fill quickly, so have a second date in mind.

2. Match the package to your guest list

Little Bounce (PHP 4,500) is a single bounce house for a smaller celebration. Ball Pit Bash (PHP 5,500) suits toddlers. Bash & Bounce (PHP 8,500) pairs a bounce house with a ball pit for a fuller party, and The Luxe Bash (PHP 12,500) adds balloon décor. See all of them on the packages page.

3. Choose your look

Pick between soft and aesthetic or bright and colorful on our styles page, then choose ball colors from the add-ons palette: White, Cream, Champagne, Blush, Sage, Mocha, Sky or Lavender. You can mix up to three.

4. Add the photo-ready extras

A balloon garland (from PHP 2,500), arch (from PHP 3,500), backdrop and signage (from PHP 2,800) or table styling (from PHP 1,800) can be ticked in the booking form.

5. Reserve and relax

Submit your booking request and pay the 50% reservation fee to secure the date. We confirm within 24 hours, then deliver, set up and supervise on the day.
