Indoor or outdoor? Choosing the best space for your bounce house

Both work. Here is how to decide, and what to check before you book.

Aug 2026 | 4 min read | By the Bounce Bash PH team

Our Dry Zone inflatables are made for year-round indoor or outdoor events, so the right choice comes down to your space.

Outdoors

Gardens, driveways and open courtyards give you the most room. In the Philippines, shade matters, so a covered area or an afternoon slot keeps kids comfortable. The Splash Zone is made for hot days outdoors.

Indoors

Function halls and large clubhouses are a great rainy-season option. Measure the doorways and the ceiling height, and let us know the layout.

Checklist

  • Flat ground or floor
  • Enough clearance on all sides
  • A way to bring the equipment in
  • Access to power for the blower
  • Shade, if outdoors

Delivery charges depend on your venue, so ask us when you check availability.
