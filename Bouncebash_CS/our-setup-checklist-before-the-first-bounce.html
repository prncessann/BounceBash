Safety

Our setup checklist: what happens before the first bounce

A look at what our crew does on arrival, so you know every party starts safe.

Sep 2026 | 5 min read | By the Bounce Bash PH team

Safety is the first thing we set up. Here is the routine our crew follows at every event.

  • Walk the space and choose a flat, clear spot
  • Clear the ground of sharp objects and debris
  • Lay down protective ground sheets
  • Set up and secure the inflatable
  • Inflate and check that it is firm and stable
  • Wipe down and set up the ball pit and décor
  • Brief the host on the play rules

Why we inspect

All our equipment is cleaned and inspected before it leaves us. Our crew checks it again on site.

On-site supervision

Every rental includes on-site supervision, so you can enjoy the party while we keep an eye on the play area.

What you can do

Share your venue details when you book, including whether the space is indoor or outdoor and where power is available. Read more in our FAQ.
