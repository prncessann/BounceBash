How to pick the right inflatable for your guest list

Guest count, ages and space all point to one setup. Here is how to read them.

Oct 2026 | 4 min read | By the Bounce Bash PH team

Choosing an inflatable is easier when you start from who is coming, not from what looks biggest.

Count the kids, not the guests

Only the children will be bouncing at any one time. Our styles list a suggested number of kids, for example up to 10 or up to 12, so use your child guest count rather than total headcount.

Think about ages

Toddlers do best with a ball pit, which is why Ball Pit Bash exists. Our Dry Zone styles are listed for ages 2 to 10 or 3 to 12, so check the age range against your group.

Mix zones for mixed ages

A bounce house and ball pit together, like Bash & Bounce, gives bigger kids something to climb and bounce on while little ones stay in the ball pit. Browse the three play zones to see what fits.

Rule of thumb

  • Under 10 kids, mostly toddlers: Ball Pit Bash
  • Smaller party, school-age kids: Little Bounce
  • Wide age range or a fuller party: Bash & Bounce
  • Want it all styled: The Luxe Bash
