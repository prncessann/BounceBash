Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about booking, fees, delivery and setup.

How do I book?

Choose a package and any add-ons, submit the booking request, then pay the 50% reservation fee to secure your date.

How much is the reservation fee?

The reservation fee is 50% of the package price. It secures your date.

How quickly will you confirm my request?

We will confirm within 24 hours.

Do you deliver and set up?

Yes. Delivery, setup and pick-up are part of every rental. Delivery charges depend on your venue, so ask us when you check availability.

Is someone there to supervise?

Yes. We provide on-site supervision with every rental.

Can I add extras later?

Add-ons are selected in the booking form. We will confirm availability and the final price with you.

Ready to add a little more bounce?

Check your date, pick a package and reserve with a 50% down payment.
